Privacy Policy

JobSearch Cold Email ("the app") is a personal tool for sending job application emails.

Data accessed

With your permission, the app accesses your Google account email address and Gmail (to send and manage emails you choose to send).

How data is used

Gmail data is used only to provide the app's features. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

Authentication tokens are stored securely and used only to operate the app on your behalf. You can revoke access anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
